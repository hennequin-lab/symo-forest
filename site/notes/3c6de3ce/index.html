The compile-time dictionary is exact, not a surrogate

#scale

The table of inner products in the inner-product construction is an exact linear dictionary between factor lists and block matrices. It is exact because of the structural fact, not because the probes were lucky.

If you like the word surrogate, this is a surrogate-matrix construction with one difference: because is forced to be the identity on everything you did not probe, the small matrix represents the big one rather than imitating it. There is no residual to minimize and no approximation error to report.

The paper's phrase for the same idea is model reduction: any analytic function of an orbit average has the same basis, and its factors can be computed in the reduced space. The reduction is exact because the discarded directions are not discarded — they are determined by symmetry (the isomorphism argument).

This is the conceptual difference between "a small matrix that approximates the operator" and "the operator's action on the part of the space that is not forced". The latter is what the dictionary gives you. It is also why shrinking the model is the wrong mental picture.
