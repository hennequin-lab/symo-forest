Preconditioning with the inverse square root of S_g

#optimization

Take the estimator and step against its inverse:

with the momentum-corrected gradient. It is a quasi-Newton step with a curvature that was never formed, and by the round trip the inverse square root comes out of eigendecompositions of matrices of size at most the free dimension rather than the parameter count.

The estimate being used is the one that drops (why S_w is dropped), not the PSD solution of the Riccati equation. That is a deliberate choice: the rank-deficient is numerically hostile, and the simpler estimate tracks the descent direction better.

Damping is relative (the relative floor), so the step is stable when the estimate is nearly singular. The zero-to-negative-power rule means a degenerate estimate produces a zero step rather than a NaN.

The update is the object the library's optimizer implements: momentum, orbit averages and the estimator in the jitted prepare, the blockwise damped powers in the host solve, and the application of the resulting factors in the jitted finish (the jit split). The two canonical special cases are Muon and Shampoo; the trailhead is the optimization trail.
