Why this formula? It is the matrix analogue of taking a ratio of square roots. It is symmetric in the right way, it reduces to in one dimension, and it is the only solution that is PSD for all PSD . The inner square root is taken of a matrix that is similar to , so it exists and is PSD whenever is.

For the optimizer, the useful object is the inverse, not itself. The library's Solve.hessian_inverse forms it with damped symmetric powers, the same formula with each power replaced by its damped version (damping).

The formula is the "correct" solution to the equation, but it is not the estimator the paper recommends. The reason is numerical, and it is the subject of why the paper sets S_w to the identity.
