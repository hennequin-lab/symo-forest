The durable part is the algebra, not the optimizer

#optimization

The author's assessment: what is fairly confident is that the durable part of this work is the algebra rather than the optimizer. An estimate you can store in numbers, invert exactly, and reason about symbolically is useful whether or not it ever replaces Adam.

The reasons are structural. The compression is exact, not approximate (the isomorphism argument). The matrix functions are exact at the model's own size (the round trip). The object can be transported between sizes (transport). None of that depends on the optimization results, and all of it is useful for the downstream consumers that want a matrix: Laplace approximations, pruning scores, continual-learning penalties, influence functions (the preconditioning use case is the last section of the source essay, not the first).

The optimization story may or may not survive careful ablation (the ablation). The algebra will still be a compact, exact description of the curvature's invariant part. That is the claim this forest is built to make checkable.
