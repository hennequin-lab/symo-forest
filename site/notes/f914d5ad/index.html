Zero runtime probing and multi-group factorization

Two implementation properties in Blocks.build are worth emphasizing:

  1. No runtime probing. The test vectors are evaluated against the basis terms only once, at compile time, to populate the small scalar matrix (at most floats for a single group). At runtime, Blocks.to_blocks and Blocks.of_blocks simply take linear combinations of the factor tensors weighted by the entries of and .
  2. Factored construction across multiple groups. When a leaf pair is acted on by independent groups , Blocks.build_group builds the per-group table on separately for each group , and Blocks.build forms the multi-group table entry for a term and global kind as the product of the per-group pairings .
