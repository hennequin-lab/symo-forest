A sub-network symmetry vs a whole-computation symmetry

#recurrence

The two groups of the RNN specification are different in kind, and the difference is what makes the picture interesting.

is a symmetry of the whole computation. The relabelling has to be applied at every time step, so it reaches every tensor that ever touches the state — the recurrence, the input map, the readout. An orbit average that respects is constrained across tensors, not just within them. That is precisely what the off-diagonal tiles in the mosaic are showing.

A useful analogy: is a gauge symmetry of one layer's internal labels; is a gauge symmetry of the whole state trajectory. The second is stronger because the state is shared by all the tensors, and it is the reason the RNN curvature is a mosaic rather than a stack of independent blocks.

The two groups are independent, so the joint constraint is the intersection of the two: a tensor touched by both groups (the two weight matrices) is constrained on both axes, by different groups. The cross-tile counts are in cross tiles.
