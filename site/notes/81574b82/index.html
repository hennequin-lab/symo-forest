The cost scales with multiplicity, not width

The point is that is a property of the kind, not of the width . For one permuted axis there are two kinds with multiplicity one each, so the cost is where is the free dimension. For signed permutations it is . For two axes tied by the same group it is times the free-dimension squared (the fifteen).

The carrier, on which the operator is forced to be the identity, is where the entries live. It is huge, and it costs nothing to store because there is nothing to store: the operator's action there is determined. This is the precise sense in which the compression is structural rather than approximate.
