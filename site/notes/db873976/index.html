You cannot form the matrix you need

#scale

For a first layer, the curvature operator has entries. For a 10M-parameter model it has entries, 400 TB in float32 (the scale). Forming it, storing it, and diagonalizing it are all out of reach.

The tempting response is to keep it implicit and use a Krylov or Chebyshev method. That gets you for a single vector , after many matvecs. It needs the operator in an iterable form, it hands you back nothing you can store or reuse, and is a noisy estimate anyway.

And a matvec is not what is wanted. The optimizer needs a matrix function — to precondition, or to estimate — not the action of on one vector. A matrix function is a categorically harder object than a matvec, because it depends on the whole spectrum.

The way out is not a cheaper approximation of . It is that was never a generic symmetric matrix: it commutes with the group, and that confines it to an algebra of low dimension (the algebra). The question becomes how to exploit that confinement without ever forming the matrix. The answer starts with the trap in the coordinate trap.
