The ensemble you didn't have to train — trailhead

#meta#ensemble

The central move of the whole framework: you have one trained model, and you want the mean and covariance of the models you would have got by relabelling it. You do not train them. The orbit is the ensemble, and the ensemble statistics are group averages.

The argument

  • The ensemble you didn't have to train — the claim, stated precisely.
  • A commuting optimizer maps orbits to orbits — the one dynamical assumption, and what violates it.
  • The orbit ensemble is narrower than a real ensemble — what the claim does and does not cover.
