Cross tiles compare indices across tensors

#recurrence

The cross tiles are the interesting ones in the RNN mosaic. Between and the equalities run across the two tensors: acts on 's hidden index and on 's hidden index simultaneously, and likewise on the two state indices.

So the tile asks: is this hidden unit in the same as that hidden unit in ? Four patterns again, and four distinct values in the figure. The same number of patterns as a diagonal tile, but a different lattice of equalities.

This is why the off-diagonal tiles are not simply transposes of anything. A transpose would answer "is this hidden unit the same as that one" within the same tensor. The cross tile answers a question that relates two different tensors through a shared group action. The values are genuinely different information: they are the cross moments , not the second moments of a single leaf.

The picture to keep: the whole-computation group ties indices across tensors, and the cross tiles are where that tying becomes visible. The extreme case of cross-tensor tying is a single group acting on both sides of a square matrix: the vanilla RNN.
