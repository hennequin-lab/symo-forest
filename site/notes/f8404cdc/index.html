Two jitted halves around a host solve

#implementation

The step is split into two jitted halves around the host-side solve:

prepare (jitted)                solve (host)              finish (jitted)
momentum, orbit averages,  →    eigh + damped        →    factors of H_inv,
dense blocks of S_w/S_g, EMA    symmetric powers          apply to ḡ, shift θ

Everything that touches a -sized tensor is traceable (and jitted); the only host-side work is the eigendecomposition of the small blocks, whose size is an axis of the weights, not of the loss landscape (the round trip). Nothing -sized is ever materialised, and nothing of size exists at any point.

The split matters for a training loop. S.Compiled.create is cheap; the first call traces and compiles, later calls replay. The whole state, the gradients and the estimator cross the boundary as input/output leaves, so a training loop never retraces. The estimator carries block coordinates rather than a surrogate, so it is exact at the model's own size; transport exists for the cases where you want to move an operator between sizes (transport).

This is the natural interface when the interesting part is a tiny eigendecomposition surrounded by huge einsums. It is also the design decision that makes the eager S.step and the compiled step numerically identical; test_jit checks fifty steps of parity and that the loop replays rather than retraces (the test suite).
