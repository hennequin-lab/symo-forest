Shampoo keeps left and right factors separate

#optimization

Shampoo's preconditioned update is

with and accumulated separately from the gradient's left and right second moments. In the framework's language it is the same kind of object as Muon, with one factor per axis rather than a single tied factor.

The difference is which symmetry is declared. Muon's signed-permutation estimate ties the two sides through one ; Shampoo keeps the left and right factors independent. That is a different point on the dial (the group is a hyperparameter), and it corresponds to a different (larger) algebra of invariant operators.

The two are not competitors in the framework's language; they are two settings of the same construction. The framework's contribution is to say what the setting is: a choice of which group the loss is declared invariant under, and therefore how long the orbit is and how small the algebra is. Shampoo's separate factors are a sliver of symmetry on each axis; Muon's polar factor is the tied version.
