Finite and continuous weight-space symmetry groups

#symmetry

Neural architectures admit several natural families of weight-space symmetries. Distinguishing the finite orthogonal groups from continuous or non-orthogonal groups clarifies which symmetries yield closed-form orthogonal projections in the partition algebra.
