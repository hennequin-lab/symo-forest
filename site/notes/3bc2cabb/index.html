The ReLU rescaling symmetry is continuous

It explains the flat directions people talk about in ReLU networks: the loss is constant along a whole manifold, not just at finitely many relabelled points. The orbit is infinite, and an orbit average would be an integral over it rather than a finite mean.

This symmetry is different in kind from the permutation cases in three ways. It is continuous, so the orbit average needs a measure and an integrability argument. It is not orthogonal in the raw parameters, so the projection formulas of this forest do not apply without a gauge choice (orthogonality). And it is a symmetry of the network, not necessarily of a loss with weight decay: an L2 penalty is not invariant under versus unless .

For these reasons the practical framework stays with the finite orthogonal groups. The rescaling symmetry is best treated as a flat direction to be quotiented or gauge-fixed, not as another entry in the probe construction. It is still worth knowing about, because it is the cleanest example of how "the loss cannot see it" can mean a manifold rather than a finite set (orbit as a level set).
