The six-step spine

  1. Orbit averages are orthogonal projections. By orthogonality of , is the Euclidean orthogonal projection onto the invariant subspace (first-order projection), and the twirl — of which is the rank-one case — is the Frobenius-orthogonal projection onto the commutant (intertwiner space) (second-order projection).

  2. The commutant is spanned by equality-pattern diagrams. For permuting left axes and right axes of dimension , an entry of can depend on those indices only through which subsets of them are equal (equality patterns). The resulting basis of sparse binary Kronecker-delta tensors is indexed by set partitions of and has dimension equal to the Bell number ( for one permuted axis, for two tied axes), tensored with the free axes (diagram terms and Bell numbers).

  3. Projection reduces to a symbolic Gram system and einsum contractions. The inner products depend only on the join in the partition lattice and are evaluated symbolically in time (symbolic Gram matrix). Cholesky-factoring once at compile time reduces computing the factor coordinates of to einsum contractions followed by two small triangular solves (compile-time projection solve).
