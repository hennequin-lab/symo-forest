full curvature

Half a million times smaller, and the inverse square root of the compressed object is exact. That is the entire technical content of the paper's pitch, as far as the author is concerned.

The numbers are not an approximation budget; they are the dimension of the algebra (the cost formula). The full curvature is because a generic symmetric matrix has that many entries. The orbit average is smaller because invariance forces most of those entries to be copies determined by a few numbers (equality patterns).

The same table for the RNN examples is in the vanilla RNN and the mosaic.
