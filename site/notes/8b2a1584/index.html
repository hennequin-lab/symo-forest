The round trip in four steps

#scale

The algorithm, in four lines:

  1. Estimate the factors of from the gradient (einsums, ).
  2. Decode them into blocks with the compile-time table (linear, ).
  3. Apply to each block ( eigendecompositions, each).
  4. Re-encode the resulting blocks into factors with the inverse table, and apply them to the momentum ().

Step 3 is the only one where the model's width does not appear. That is the punchline: the expensive, nonlinear part of the computation is small, and the large parts are linear contractions that can be jitted.

The round trip was checked on the example: computing densely on the operator and computing it via the two blocks were reported to agree to . That is the numerical evidence that the dictionary and the algebra isomorphism are doing what they claim.

The trap this avoids is the coordinate trap; the warning about a superficially similar shortcut is the shrink warning; the implementation of the split is the jit split.
