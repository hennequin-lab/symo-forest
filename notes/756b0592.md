# The commutant and partition algebra — trailhead

By [[8eb163bd|the twirl projection theorem]], the second-order orbit average $cal(R)_2(bold(u), bold(v))$ is the Frobenius-orthogonal projection of $bold(u) bold(v)^top$ onto the commutant $cal(C)(W_R, W_L)$. This trail constructs the explicit combinatorial basis of $cal(C)(W_R, W_L)$ for permutation groups $cal(S)_n$: the **diagram basis** of the partition algebra, indexed by index-equality patterns and counted by Bell numbers. It then shows how the Gram matrix of this non-orthogonal basis is evaluated symbolically on the partition lattice and Cholesky-factored once at compile time.

## Notes in this trail

- [[273c28e5|First and second moments of a random permutation matrix]] — closed-form derivation of $bb(E)[bold(P)_sigma ⊗ bold(P)_sigma]$ in terms of Kronecker deltas and the orthogonal projectors $bold(Pi), bold(Pi)_perp$.
- [[33d479c5|Index invariance and the equality-pattern basis]] — why $cal(S)_n$-invariance forces matrix entries to depend only on which permuted indices coincide, yielding the two-term $delta / 1$ expansion for $k_L = k_R = 1$.
- [[57bf40fa|Worked example: a 3×4 weight matrix under three symmetry groups]] — concrete parameter counts and matrix structures for a $12 times 12$ operator under $cal(S)_3$ ($32$ numbers), $cal(B)_3$ ($16$ numbers), and $cal(S)_3 times cal(S)_4$ ($4$ numbers).
- [[d47131ad|The Π/Π⊥ projector basis and the row mean–covariance identity]] — change of basis from $(delta, 1)$ to $(bold(Pi), bold(Pi)_perp)$ and proof that the two blocks of $cal(R)_2(bold(c), bold(c))$ are the outer product of the mean row and the sample covariance of the rows.
- [[a0bb3d95|Partial partitions, diagram terms, and Bell numbers]] — generalization to $k_L + k_R$ permuted axes: set partitions, partial partitions (`Term.t`), Bell numbers $B_(k_L + k_R)$, and basis normalization.
- [[b81e35a1|Symbolic Gram matrix via the partition lattice]] — why the diagram basis is non-orthogonal and how `Term.inner_product` computes $bold(G)_(P Q) = ⟨bold(T)_P, bold(T)_Q⟩_F$ symbolically in $O(1)$ time via the partition join $P or Q$.
- [[e176661b|Projecting outer products via compile-time Cholesky and einsums]] — solving the normal equations $bold(G) bold(c) = bold(b)$ with a compile-time Cholesky factor and $O(D)$ split einsum contractions.
- [[4875a459|Symmetric component bundling and the Λ²W ⊗ Λ²F completeness condition]] — when bundling a diagram term with its transpose ($bold(T)_P + bold(T)_P^top$) spans the symmetric commutant and when the compiler must retain the full basis (`Compiler.symmetric_basis_is_complete`).
