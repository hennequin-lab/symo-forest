# Weight-space symmetries and orbit projections — trailhead

A **weight-space symmetry** of a parameterized model $f(bold(w), bold(x))$ is a group $cal(G)$ acting on the parameter space $W$ via an orthogonal representation $rho : cal(G) -> O(W)$ that leaves the model's input–output map (and hence any data-dependent loss $cal(L) : W -> RR$) unchanged:
```typst
cal(L)(rho(g) bold(w)) = cal(L)(bold(w)) quad forall g in cal(G), bold(w) in W.
```
This trail develops the representation of permutation and sign-flip symmetries on multi-tensor parameter trees, derives the equivariance of derivatives along the group orbit, and proves that the first- and second-order orbit averages $cal(R)_1$ and $cal(R)_2$ are exact orthogonal projections onto the fixed-point subspace $W^(cal(G))$ and the commutant algebra $cal(C)$.
