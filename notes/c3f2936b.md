# Diagonal and cross-leaf tiles in the multi-leaf commutant

For a parameter tree $W = ⊕_(i=1)^L W_i$ of total dimension $D = sum_(i=1)^L D_i$, any operator $bold(S) in cal(C)(W, W)$ decomposes into an $L times L$ grid of rectangular **tiles** $bold(S)_(i j) : W_j -> W_i$ of size $D_i times D_j$. By [[8eb163bd|the twirl definition]], the $(i, j)$ tile of the second-order orbit average $cal(R)_2(bold(u), bold(v))$ is the cross-leaf intertwiner
```typst
bold(S)_(i j) = cal(R)_2 (bold(u)_i, bold(v)_j) = bb(E)_(g in cal(G)) [ (rho_i (g) bold(u)_i) (rho_j (g) bold(v)_j)^top ] in cal(C)(W_j, W_i).
```
Because multiple leaves share the same group action ($rho_i(g)$ and $rho_j(g)$ are driven by the same permutation $g in cal(G)$), the off-diagonal tiles $bold(S)_(i j)$ ($i != j$) do *not* factor into products of first-order averages: they capture the exact group-invariant cross-correlations between leaf $i$ and leaf $j$.

## Combinatorial value counts in the MLP-step RNN mosaic

In the $7$-leaf [[a97e31ca|MLP-step recurrent network]] with symmetry group $cal(G) = cal(G)_h times cal(G)_z = cal(S)_(n_h) times cal(S)_(n_z)$, the dimension of $cal(C)(W_j, W_i)$ — and the number of distinct entry values inside each tile $bold(S)_(i j)$ — is determined by the per-group axis counts $(k_(i,h) + k_(j,h), k_(i,z) + k_(j,z))$ and the free dimensions $f_i, f_j$:
```typst
dim cal(C)(W_j, W_i) = B_(k_(i,h) + k_(j,h)) dot B_(k_(i,z) + k_(j,z)) dot f_i f_j,
```
where $B_k$ is the [[a0bb3d95|Bell number]] ($B_0 = 1, B_1 = 1, B_2 = 2$).

### 1. Diagonal transition tiles $bold(S)_(1 1) in cal(C)(W_1, W_1)$ and $bold(S)_(2 2) in cal(C)(W_2, W_2)$ ($4$ distinct values)

On $bold(W)_1 in RR^(n_h times n_z)$, row indices $m, o in {1, dots, n_h}$ are permuted by $cal(G)_h$ ($k_(1,h) = 1$) and column indices $n, p in {1, dots, n_z}$ are permuted independently by $cal(G)_z$ ($k_(1,z) = 1$), with $f_1 = 1$. Thus $k_(1,h) + k_(1,h) = 2$ and $k_(1,z) + k_(1,z) = 2$, giving $B_2 dot B_2 = 2 times 2 = 4$ basis tensors:
```typst
(bold(S)_(1 1))_((m,n),(o,p))
= c_1 delta_(m o)^h delta_(n p)^z + c_2 delta_(m o)^h 1_(n p)^z + c_3 1_(m o)^h delta_(n p)^z + c_4 1_(m o)^h 1_(n p)^z.
```
Every entry of the $(n_h n_z) times (n_h n_z)$ tile takes one of at most **four distinct scalar values**, indexed by the two independent equality predicates $(m = o)$ and $(n = p)$:
- $(m = o, n = p)$: $c_1 + c_2 + c_3 + c_4$ ($n_h n_z$ diagonal entries),
- $(m = o, n != p)$: $c_2 + c_4$ ($n_h n_z(n_z - 1)$ entries),
- $(m != o, n = p)$: $c_3 + c_4$ ($n_h(n_h - 1) n_z$ entries),
- $(m != o, n != p)$: $c_4$ ($n_h(n_h - 1) n_z(n_z - 1)$ entries).

The same count holds for $bold(S)_(2 2)$ on $bold(W)_2 in RR^(n_z times n_h)$, regardless of how large $n_h$ and $n_z$ are.

### 2. Cross-transition tile $bold(S)_(1 2) in cal(C)(W_2, W_1)$ ($4$ distinct values)

The off-diagonal tile $bold(S)_(1 2) = cal(R)_2(bold(u)_1, bold(v)_2)$ pairs an entry $(m, n)$ of $bold(W)_1 in RR^(n_h times n_z)$ ($m in {1, dots, n_h}$, $n in {1, dots, n_z}$) against an entry $(p, o)$ of $bold(W)_2 in RR^(n_z times n_h)$ ($p in {1, dots, n_z}$, $o in {1, dots, n_h}$). Here $cal(G)_h$ permutes the first axis $m$ of $bold(W)_1$ *simultaneously* with the second axis $o$ of $bold(W)_2$, while $cal(G)_z$ permutes the second axis $n$ of $bold(W)_1$ simultaneously with the first axis $p$ of $bold(W)_2$:
```typst
(bold(S)_(1 2))_((m,n),(p,o))
= c'_1 delta_(m o)^h delta_(n p)^z + c'_2 delta_(m o)^h 1_(n p)^z + c'_3 1_(m o)^h delta_(n p)^z + c'_4 1_(m o)^h 1_(n p)^z.
```
Again there are $dim cal(C)(W_2, W_1) = B_2 dot B_2 = 4$ basis tensors and **four distinct scalar values**, but the equality ties $(delta_(m o)^h, delta_(n p)^z)$ now match row indices of $bold(W)_1$ against column indices of $bold(W)_2$, and column indices of $bold(W)_1$ against row indices of $bold(W)_2$. Because $(m, n)$ and $(p, o)$ have transposed axis orderings, the sparsity pattern of $bold(T)_1 = delta_(m o)^h delta_(n p)^z$ inside the $(n_h n_z) times (n_z n_h)$ tile $bold(S)_(1 2)$ is the vector-transpose commutation matrix rather than the identity diagonal.

### 3. Bias and weight-to-bias tiles ($1$ or $2$ values per free entry)

- **Diagonal bias tiles $bold(S)_(3 3)$ on $bold(b)_1 in RR^(n_h)$ and $bold(S)_(4 4)$ on $bold(b)_2 in RR^(n_z)$:** Each has one permuted axis and $f = 1$, so $dim cal(C) = B_2 = 2$. The tile is $a bold(I) + b bold(J)$, having **two distinct values** (one on the diagonal, one off the diagonal).
- **Cross tile between $bold(W)_1$ and $bold(b)_1$ ($bold(S)_(1 3) in cal(C)(W_3, W_1)$):** Here $k_(1,h) + k_(3,h) = 1 + 1 = 2$ and $k_(1,z) + k_(3,z) = 1 + 0 = 1$, so $dim cal(C)(W_3, W_1) = B_2 dot B_1 = 2 times 1 = 2$: the entry $((m, n), o)$ depends only on whether $m = o$ in $cal(G)_h$ and is constant across $n in {1, dots, n_z}$ because $B_1 = 1$ forces $cal(G)_z$ to average uniformly over $n$.
- **Cross tile between $bold(b)_1$ and $bold(b)_2$ ($bold(S)_(3 4) in cal(C)(W_4, W_3)$):** Here $k_(3,h) + k_(4,h) = 1 + 0 = 1$ and $k_(3,z) + k_(4,z) = 0 + 1 = 1$, so $dim cal(C)(W_4, W_3) = B_1 dot B_1 = 1$: the entire $n_h times n_z$ cross tile is a constant multiple of the all-ones matrix $bold(1)_(n_h) bold(1)_(n_z)^top$.

How all $7 times 7 = 49$ tiles of this mosaic are assembled into four small square matrices — one for each global kind $(lambda_h, lambda_z) in {emptyset, [1]} times {emptyset, [1]}$ — is shown in [[a575e53a|multi-group product kinds and tree block assembly]].
