# The compile-time dictionary between factor coordinates and Wedderburn blocks

Combining [[a0bb3d95|the diagram basis expansion]] $bold(S) = sum_(k=1)^d bold(T)_k ⊗ bold(C)_k$ with [[0c051391|the pairing theorem]] yields a static, compile-time linear dictionary between factor lists $bold(c) = (bold(C)_1, dots, bold(C)_d)$ and Wedderburn blocks $(bold(B)_lambda)_lambda$, implemented in `murder/packages/symo/lib/blocks.ml`.

## Corollary (the compile-time conversion table)

Let ${bold(T)_1, dots, bold(T)_d}$ be the compiled components of the commutant on the permuted axes of a leaf pair $(W_R, W_L)$, and let $bold(z)_(lambda a)^L in V^(⊗k_L)$ and $bold(z)_(lambda b)^R in V^(⊗k_R)$ be the [[7ee7cea7|canonical test vectors]] for each common kind $lambda$ and copies $a in {1, dots, m_lambda^L}$, $b in {1, dots, m_lambda^R}$.

Define the **raw pairing table** $bold(P)_"raw" in RR^(n_"entries" times d)$ (where $n_"entries" = sum_lambda m_lambda^L m_lambda^R$ is the total number of block entries across all common kinds) and the **Gram-corrected dictionary table** $bold(P) in RR^(n_"entries" times d)$ (`Blocks.table`) by
```typst
(bold(P)_"raw")_((lambda, a, b), k) = ⟨bold(z)_(lambda a)^L, bold(T)_k bold(z)_(lambda b)^R⟩,
quad quad
bold(P)_((lambda, a, b), k) = sum_(a'=1)^(m_lambda^L) ((bold(G)_lambda^L)^(-1))_(a a') (bold(P)_"raw")_((lambda, a', b), k).
```
Then for any operator $bold(S) = sum_(k=1)^d bold(T)_k ⊗ bold(C)_k in cal(C)(W_R, W_L)$ with factor matrices $bold(C)_k in RR^(f_L times f_R)$:
1. **Decoding factors to blocks (`Blocks.to_blocks`):** The $(a, b)$ block slice $(bold(B)_lambda)_(a b) in RR^(f_L times f_R)$ of $bold(B)_lambda in RR^((m_lambda^L f_L) times (m_lambda^R f_R))$ is given exactly by
   ```typst
   (bold(B)_lambda)_(a b) = sum_(k=1)^d bold(P)_((lambda, a, b), k) bold(C)_k.
   ```
2. **Encoding blocks to factors (`Blocks.of_blocks`):** Let $bold(P)^dagger in RR^(d times n_"entries")$ (`Blocks.inverse`) be the Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse of $bold(P)$ (computed in `float64` with relative tolerance $10^(-10)$). Then the factor matrices are recovered from the blocks by
   ```typst
   bold(C)_k = sum_(lambda, a, b) (bold(P)^dagger)_(k, (lambda, a, b)) (bold(B)_lambda)_(a b).
   ```
Both maps are **exact linear isomorphisms** in [[b50cfe81|the stable range]] $n >= 2 max(k_L, k_R)$: $bold(P)^dagger bold(P) = bold(I)_d$ on the factor space, and $bold(P) bold(P)^dagger$ is the identity on the block space (or on the symmetric block space when compiled symmetrically).

### Proof

By linearity of the inner product and [[0c051391|the pairing theorem]],
```typst
sum_(k=1)^d (bold(P)_"raw")_((lambda, a', b), k) bold(C)_k
= ⟨bold(z)_(lambda a')^L, bold(S) bold(z)_(lambda b)^R⟩
= (bold(G)_lambda^L bold(B)_lambda)_(a' b),
```
where the pairing is applied entrywise across the free axes $F_L, F_R$. Multiplying on the left by $(bold(G)_lambda^L)^(-1)$ yields $(bold(B)_lambda)_(a b) = sum_(k=1)^d bold(P)_((lambda, a, b), k) bold(C)_k$.

In the asymmetric compilation, $n_"entries" = sum_lambda m_lambda^L m_lambda^R = d = B_(k_L + k_R)$ ($2 times 2$ for $k = 1$, $15 times 15$ for $k = 2$), and since both the diagram basis and the Wedderburn blocks form bases of $cal(C)(V^(⊗k_R), V^(⊗k_L))$, $bold(P)$ is a square invertible matrix and $bold(P)^dagger = bold(P)^(-1)$. In the symmetric compilation of a diagonal pair ($k_L = k_R = 2, f = 1$), $n_"entries" = 15$ while $d = 11$: the four off-diagonal copy pairs $(a, b)$ and $(b, a)$ ($a != b$) in $bold(B)_emptyset$ ($2 times 2$) and $bold(B)_([1])$ ($3 times 3$) carry equal entries $(bold(B)_lambda)_(a b) = (bold(B)_lambda)_(b a)$, so $bold(P) in RR^(15 times 11)$ has full column rank $11$, $bold(P)^dagger bold(P) = bold(I)_(11)$, and $bold(P) bold(P)^dagger$ is the identity on the $11$-dimensional subspace of symmetric blocks. $square$

## Zero runtime probing and multi-group factorization

Two implementation properties in `Blocks.build` are worth emphasizing:
1. **No runtime probing.** The test vectors $bold(z)_(lambda a)$ are evaluated against the basis terms $bold(T)_k$ *only once, at compile time*, to populate the small scalar matrix $bold(P) in RR^(n_"entries" times d)$ (at most $15 times 15 = 225$ floats for a single $k = 2$ group). At runtime, `Blocks.to_blocks` and `Blocks.of_blocks` simply take linear combinations of the $f_L times f_R$ factor tensors weighted by the entries of $bold(P)$ and $bold(P)^dagger$.
2. **Factored construction across multiple groups.** When a leaf pair is acted on by $G$ independent groups $cal(G)_1, dots, cal(G)_G$, `Blocks.build_group` builds the per-group table $bold(P)^((g))$ on $RR^(n_g^(k_(L,g)))$ separately for each group $g = 1, dots, G$, and `Blocks.build` forms the multi-group table entry for a term $bold(T) = ⊗_(g=1)^G bold(T)^((g))$ and global kind $bold(lambda) = (lambda_1, dots, lambda_G)$ as the product of the per-group pairings $product_(g=1)^G bold(P)_((lambda_g, a_g, b_g), k_g)^((g))$. Thus even multi-group leaves never materialize joint test vectors of size $product_g n_g^(k_g)$.
