# Module correspondence and algebraic verification in symo

The module structure of `murder/packages/symo/lib/` mirrors the mathematical progression from index partitions to multi-leaf Wedderburn blocks.

## Numerical precision and purity conventions

1. **Float64 compile-time factorizations, float32 runtime tensors.** Runtime parameter trees, factor lists, and block matrices are `float32` tensors so that contractions execute at hardware throughput. Compile-time matrix inversions that may be sensitive to conditioning — the Cholesky factorization (and eigendecomposition pseudoinverse fallback) of the symbolic Gram matrix $bold(G)$ in `Compiler.build_estimator`, the copy Gram inverse $(bold(G)_lambda^L)^(-1)$ and Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse $bold(P)^dagger$ in `Blocks.build`, and block eigendecompositions in `Solve` — cast to `float64` before factoring and cast the resulting factors back to `float32`.
2. **Explicit functional PRNG keys.** No module reads global mutable random state; `Orbit.S.random_transform` takes an explicit `Nx.Rng.key` and derives per-group permutations via `Nx.Rng.fold_in key id` and `argsort`, ensuring deterministic, JIT-traceable group sampling.

## Algebraic verification suite (`murder/packages/symo/test/`)

The algebraic claims of the vault are verified by four unit test suites that cross-check the symbolic and block algorithms against brute-force enumeration at small dimensions:

- **`test_term`:** Verifies canonical sorting and equality of partial partitions, transposition $bold(T)_P^top = bold(T)_(P^top)$, and exact agreement between the $O(1)$ [[b81e35a1|symbolic partition-join inner product]] `Term.inner_product` and explicit dense Frobenius inner products over $n^(k_L + k_R)$ index grids.
- **`test_compiler`:** Verifies that the compiled einsum closures `estimate_factors` and `apply_block` match brute-force index-space contractions, that the projected operator $bold(S)$ satisfies the intertwining relation $rho_L(g) bold(S) rho_R(g)^top = bold(S)$ for random permutations $g in cal(G)$, and that the projection is idempotent ($cal(T)^2 = cal(T)$) and Frobenius-optimal.
- **`test_orbit`:** Enumerates all $|cal(G)| = product_a (n_a !)$ elements of the symmetry group on small multi-leaf parameter trees and verifies that `First_order.orbit_average` and `Second_order.dense_of_factors (factors_of_pair u v)` agree to machine precision with the exact group averages $frac(1, |cal(G)|)sum_(g in cal(G)) rho(g) bold(u)$ and $frac(1, |cal(G)|)sum_(g in cal(G)) (rho(g) bold(u))(rho(g) bold(v))^top$.
- **`test_blocks`:** Verifies that the round trip `factors_of_blocks (blocks_of_factors c)` is the identity on $cal(C)$, that the eigenvalues of the assembled [[a575e53a|Wedderburn blocks]] $bold(B)_(bold(lambda))$ repeated with carrier multiplicities $d_(bold(lambda))(n)$ match the dense $D times D$ eigenspectrum of $bold(S)$, that [[93e6cfab|block matrix functions]] $f(bold(B)_(bold(lambda)))$ match dense matrix functions $f(bold(S))$, and that [[be071dc0|width transport]] $tau_(n -> n')$ preserves the blocks and distinct eigenvalues across widths.
